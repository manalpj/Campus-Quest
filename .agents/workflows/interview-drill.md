# /interview-drill
Quiz me on the code we just wrote.
1. Ask me ONE question at a time about the last change (how does it work, why this design, what breaks if...).
2. Wait for my answer. Do not answer for me.
3. Grade my answer in two lines: what was right, what was missing.
4. After five questions, give me a score out of 5 and list the topics I should re-read.
