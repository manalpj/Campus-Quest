Privacy Notice

Plain language disclosure of data collection, storage, and player rights.

1. What We Collect

When you register and play Campus Quest, we collect only the minimum information needed to operate the game:

  • Team Name & Member Names: Plain names provided by your team during registration to identify your group and verify winners. We do not ask for phone numbers, email addresses, or student roll numbers.
  • Device Location: GPS coordinates (latitude, longitude) and estimated accuracy sent by your phone while the browser tab is open and the game is running.
  • Device Cookie: A secure, random token stored in an HttpOnly cookie named quest_device. This cookie is required to bind your registered team to your phone so only one device tracks your progress.

2. Why We Collect It

  • Checkpoints: The server compares your GPS position against checkpoint zones using the Haversine formula to unlock clues when you arrive.
  • Fair Play: We perform basic speed plausibility checks between consecutive pings to prevent location spoofing.
  • Live Standings: Completion times and cleared checkpoints calculate the live leaderboard.

3. Where Data is Stored

All game data is stored in a private SQLite database on the event server hosted on a persistent volume. We only store your latest position; previous coordinates are overwritten on each ping. We do not record or retain a historical GPS trail or map breadcrumbs of where you walked.

4. Who Can See Your Information

  • Public Leaderboard: Displays only your team name, rank, cleared checkpoints count, and adjusted time. Member names and coordinates are never displayed publicly.
  • Organizers: Event coordinators have access to the administrative panel to manage checkpoints, verify team presence, and assist stuck teams. Organizers can see team locations during the game on a live team map, and positions are cleared after the event. Organizers can export team names and results to verify winners and final standings.

5. OpenStreetMap & Map Tiles

Map tiles are loaded directly from OpenStreetMap servers (tile.openstreetmap.org). Because your browser requests map images directly from OpenStreetMap, OpenStreetMap sees your phone's IP address and browser headers in accordance with their standard tile usage policy.

6. Data Deletion

All team registrations, member names, device tokens, stored team positions, progress logs, and event records are strictly temporary. Stored team positions are cleared after the event (and whenever an event or game is reset). After the orientation event concludes, organizers wipe all event and player data from the server. Any files exported by organizers containing team names and results should be deleted after the event.
