Event Rules & Terms

Guidelines for a safe, fair, and fun campus clue hunt.

1. Public Outdoor Areas Only

All checkpoints are located outdoors on ground level within the DAVV campus. Participants must never enter locked buildings, laboratories, faculty offices, student hostels, or restricted maintenance areas.

2. Daytime Participation & Safety

The quest takes place exclusively during daylight hours. Please stay aware of your physical surroundings at all times:

  • Watch your step around curbs, steps, and uneven pathways.
  • Look out for vehicular and pedestrian traffic when crossing campus roads.
  • Do not run with your eyes locked to your phone screen—stop to read clues.
  • Stay hydrated and alert an organizer immediately if anyone in your group feels unwell.

3. Fair Play & Anti-Cheat

  • On Foot: Teams must travel on foot. The use of motorbikes, cars, scooters, or bicycles is strictly prohibited.
  • No GPS Spoofing: Location mock apps, simulated GPS coordinates, or developer tools are prohibited during the live game. Speeds exceeding human walking pace are rejected.
  • One Phone per Team: Your team's progress is tied to a single registered phone. Splitting up across multiple active devices is not permitted.

4. Organizers' Decisions are Final

Event coordinators and volunteers are present on campus to ensure fair play. Any disputes, scoring adjustments, or disqualifications are determined solely by event organizers, and their decisions are final.
